I am a final-year engineering student at Mines Paris – PSL and an MSc student in Machine Learning and Applied Mathematics (MVA) at ENS Paris-Saclay. My main research interests are generative models, particularly discrete diffusion and flow-based language models, and understanding what drives their generalization and creativity.

I previously worked on discrete generative models at Inria / ENS Lyon, advised by Quentin Bertrand and Mathurin Massias, and in Gauthier Gidel’s group at Mila. Earlier, I developed knowledge-graph-augmented enterprise search at Artefact and worked on inverse design of bistable origami in Katia Bertoldi’s group at Harvard.

I am seeking a 5–6-month ML research internship starting in April 2027, with the aim of pursuing a PhD afterward.
