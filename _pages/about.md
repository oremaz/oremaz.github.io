I am a final-year engineering student at **Mines Paris – PSL** and an MSc student in **Machine Learning and Applied Mathematics (MVA)** at **ENS Paris-Saclay**. My main research interests are generative models, particularly **discrete diffusion and flow matching**, and understanding what drives their behavior and generalization.

I previously worked on discrete generative models at **Inria / ENS Lyon**, advised by Quentin Bertrand and Mathurin Massias, and on analytical denoisers and locality in **Gauthier Gidel’s group at Mila**. Earlier, I developed knowledge-graph-augmented enterprise search at **Artefact** and worked on inverse design of bistable origami in **Katia Bertoldi’s group at Harvard**.

I am seeking a **5–6-month ML research internship starting in April 2027**, with the aim of pursuing a PhD afterward.

### Research experience

- **Mila · Jul–Sep 2026:** Locality-constrained analytical denoisers for discrete data; linear and quadratic approximations to neural generation.
- **Inria / ENS Lyon · Jan–Jun 2026:** Benchmarking autoregressive, discrete diffusion and continuous flow models on images, Sudoku, DNA and text, using Jean Zay.
- **Artefact · May–Dec 2025:** LLM-based knowledge extraction and hybrid semantic/vector retrieval for enterprise search.
- **Harvard · Sep 2024–Feb 2025:** GPU-vectorized inverse design in JAX, with experimental validation on origami prototypes.
