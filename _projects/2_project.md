A research sandbox for AI-generated text and image detection, with retained text experiments on Jean Zay.

**Key ideas tested**

- RL-based text evasion against individual detectors and a detector ensemble.
- Detector recovery through one supervised update on adversarial outputs.
- Trade-offs between attack detection, human false positives and retention on base-model outputs.
